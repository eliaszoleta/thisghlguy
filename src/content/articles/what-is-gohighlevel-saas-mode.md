GoHighLevel's SaaS Mode is the feature that has helped hundreds of agency owners build six and seven-figure recurring revenue businesses — often without hiring a single developer.

The concept is surprisingly simple: GoHighLevel lets you take the entire platform, put your own brand on it, and resell it to clients as your own software product.

This guide explains exactly how SaaS Mode works, what it costs, and how to turn it into a real revenue stream.

## What is GoHighLevel SaaS Mode?

SaaS Mode is a feature available on GoHighLevel's **Agency Unlimited ($297/mo)** and **Agency Pro ($497/mo)** plans that allows you to:

1. **White-label** GoHighLevel with your own brand name, logo, colors, and domain
2. **Create pricing plans** for your clients (e.g. $97/mo, $297/mo, $497/mo)
3. **Automatically provision sub-accounts** when clients sign up and pay
4. **Keep 100% of the revenue** you collect from clients

GoHighLevel becomes your backend infrastructure. Your clients see your branded platform — they have no idea GoHighLevel exists underneath.

## How GoHighLevel SaaS Mode Works

Here's the full picture of what happens when you activate SaaS Mode:

### Step 1: White-Label the Platform

You connect a custom domain (e.g. `app.youragency.com`), upload your logo, set your brand colors, and write your own platform name. GoHighLevel disappears from the interface entirely.

### Step 2: Build Your SaaS Plans

Using GoHighLevel's SaaS configurator, you create pricing tiers. For example:

- **Basic Plan**: $97/month — includes CRM, email, and calendar
- **Pro Plan**: $197/month — adds SMS, funnels, and automation
- **Premium Plan**: $397/month — full access to everything

You choose which GHL features to include in each tier.

### Step 3: Load Your Snapshots

A GoHighLevel Snapshot is a pre-built package of funnels, automation workflows, pipeline stages, email templates, and settings that you can instantly deploy into any new sub-account.

Instead of manually setting up each new client from scratch, you load a Snapshot and the entire setup is done in minutes. This is how SaaS Mode becomes scalable.

### Step 4: Connect Stripe and Start Accepting Payments

GoHighLevel integrates directly with Stripe. Your clients pay through your branded checkout, and the money hits your Stripe account. GoHighLevel handles billing, upgrades, and cancellations automatically.

### Step 5: Sell and Onboard Clients

You market your SaaS product, clients sign up, pay via Stripe, and they're automatically provisioned their own branded sub-account. You collect MRR. GoHighLevel handles the software.

## What You Can White-Label

With SaaS Mode, you can brand:

- The web dashboard (your domain, your logo)
- Email notifications (sent from your domain)
- Help documentation (optionally, with Agency Pro)
- Mobile app (Agency Pro only — iOS and Android)
- Support chat branding

## The Economics of GoHighLevel SaaS Mode

Let's look at the numbers with a real example:

**Your costs:**
- GoHighLevel Agency Unlimited: $297/month
- Usage fees (email, SMS, calls): ~$50/month (scales with clients)
- **Total: ~$347/month**

**Revenue example:**
- 20 clients at $197/month = **$3,940/month**
- 30 clients at $197/month = **$5,910/month**
- 50 clients at $197/month = **$9,850/month**

**Profit margin at 50 clients:** ~$9,503/month on ~$347 in costs.

Obviously, you'll have other business costs (marketing, support, etc.), but the software margin is exceptional. This is why GHL SaaS Mode has become one of the most popular agency revenue streams.

## Who Should Use GoHighLevel SaaS Mode?

SaaS Mode makes the most sense if you:

- Already run a marketing agency and want to add software revenue on top of service revenue
- Want to build a niche-specific SaaS product (e.g. a CRM for dentists, a marketing platform for gyms)
- Have clients who need tools like CRM, email, and booking, and would pay for access
- Want to move from trading time for money toward scalable recurring revenue
