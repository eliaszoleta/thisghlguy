Keap (formerly Infusionsoft) and GoHighLevel both claim to be all-in-one CRM and marketing platforms — but they're built for different audiences with different workflows and different price points.

This comparison breaks down every major category so you can make an informed decision.

## Quick Summary

| | GoHighLevel | Keap |
|---|---|---|
| **Best for** | Marketing agencies, SaaS builders | Small businesses, solopreneurs |
| **Starting price** | $97/month | $299/month |
| **Contact limits** | Generous / unlimited (higher plans) | 1,500–25,000 contacts (tiered) |
| **White label** | Yes | No |
| **SMS marketing** | Native | Limited |
| **Funnel builder** | Yes | Basic |
| **Agency sub-accounts** | Yes | No |
| **SaaS Mode** | Yes | No |
| **Free trial** | 14 days | 14 days |

**Bottom line:** GoHighLevel offers more for less. Keap is better for businesses that want simplicity and have straightforward CRM needs.

---

## Background: What Is Keap?

Keap is a CRM and marketing automation platform that has been around since 2001 (originally as Infusionsoft). It rebranded to Keap in 2019 to simplify its product lineup.

Keap targets small business owners who want to automate follow-up, manage contacts, and process sales — without the complexity of enterprise CRMs. It's known for its automation sequences (called Campaigns) and its contact management system.

Keap has three main products:
- **Keap Pro** — CRM + automation for growing small businesses
- **Keap Max** — Advanced CRM with e-commerce and reporting
- **Keap Max Classic** (formerly Infusionsoft) — Legacy product for power users

---

## Background: What Is GoHighLevel?

GoHighLevel launched in 2018 specifically to serve marketing agencies. It's an all-in-one platform that combines CRM, marketing automation, funnel building, SMS marketing, calendar booking, reputation management, and white-label SaaS capabilities.

Unlike Keap, GoHighLevel is built around the concept of multi-client management — agencies can run hundreds of client accounts from one master dashboard.

## Pricing Comparison

### Keap Pricing

| Plan | Price | Contacts |
|---|---|---|
| Keap Pro | $299/month | 1,500 contacts |
| Keap Max | $399/month | 2,500 contacts |
| Additional contacts | Extra fee | Per 1,000 added |

Keap's pricing scales with contact count. At 10,000 contacts, you'll pay significantly more than the base price. Many users find Keap's cost increases quickly as their list grows.

### GoHighLevel Pricing

| Plan | Price | Contacts |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $97/month | 1 account |
| Agency Unlimited | $297/month | Unlimited accounts |
| SaaS Pro | $497/month | Unlimited + SaaS Mode |

GoHighLevel charges per account structure, not per contact. Agencies managing 20 clients all pay the same $297/month — contacts don't inflate the cost.

**Verdict:** GoHighLevel is dramatically more cost-effective for agencies and businesses with large contact lists.

## CRM and Contact Management

### Keap CRM

Keap has a mature CRM with solid contact management. You can:
- Store detailed contact records with custom fields
- Track email and call history
- Segment contacts with tags
- View visual sales pipelines
- Score leads automatically

Keap's CRM strength is its deep contact history and the breadth of custom fields available. It's been refined over 20+ years.

### GoHighLevel CRM

GoHighLevel's CRM is newer but covers all the fundamentals:
- Contact records with custom fields
- Conversation inbox (email, SMS, calls, DMs in one view)
- Pipeline management with drag-and-drop boards
- Tagging and smart lists
- Lead scoring (via automations)

GoHighLevel adds one thing Keap doesn't: **a unified inbox**. All contact conversations — email, SMS, Facebook Messenger, Instagram DMs, Google Business messages — appear in one feed. This is a major advantage for agencies managing lead conversations at scale.

**Verdict:** Keap has deeper individual contact history. GoHighLevel has a better multi-channel inbox and is better at managing leads across many clients.

---

## Marketing Automation

### Keap Automation (Campaigns)

Keap's Campaign Builder is its flagship feature and historically where it outshone competitors. It's a visual automation builder where you connect triggers and sequences using a canvas.

Keap excels at:
- Complex multi-branch email sequences
- Contact tagging and segmentation
- Automated follow-up based on behavior
- E-commerce order-based triggers

The Campaign Builder has a steep learning curve but is very powerful for pure email automation.

### GoHighLevel Automation (Workflows)

GoHighLevel's Workflow builder is more modern and visually cleaner than Keap's Campaign builder. It supports:

- Email sequences
- SMS sequences (Keap's SMS is limited by comparison)
- Voicemail drops
- Social media DM automation
- Conditional branching
- Wait steps by time or event
- Multi-channel sequences combining SMS + email + calls

The biggest edge: **SMS is native and first-class in GoHighLevel**. Keap's SMS capabilities are limited and require additional setup. For businesses doing lead follow-up with SMS (which has much higher open rates than email), this is a significant advantage.

**Verdict:** Keap is stronger for complex email-only automation. GoHighLevel is stronger for multi-channel automation, especially SMS.

---

## Funnel and Landing Page Builder

### Keap

Keap has basic landing page functionality — you can create simple opt-in pages — but it's not a full funnel builder. For serious funnel building, Keap users typically need to integrate ClickFunnels or Leadpages.

### GoHighLevel

GoHighLevel includes a full drag-and-drop funnel builder with:
- Multi-step funnels
- Order forms with Stripe integration
- Upsells and downsells
- A/B split testing
- Custom domains
- Membership sites

This is a major category win for GoHighLevel. Keap simply doesn't compete here.

**Verdict:** GoHighLevel wins decisively.

## Email Marketing

Both platforms support email marketing with templates, broadcasts, and sequences.

| Feature | GoHighLevel | Keap |
|---|---|---|
| Email broadcasts | Yes | Yes |
| Automated sequences | Yes | Yes (very strong) |
| Email templates | Yes | Yes (more polished) |
| Deliverability | Good | Good |
| A/B testing | Basic | Yes |
| Visual email builder | Yes | Yes |

Keap's email editor is slightly more polished. GoHighLevel's email deliverability has improved significantly and is now competitive.

**Verdict:** Roughly equal, with Keap having a slight edge in email depth.

---

## SMS Marketing

This is one of the clearest category wins in this comparison.

**GoHighLevel:** SMS is native, fully integrated, supports two-way conversation, A2P 10DLC compliance built-in, and works seamlessly in automations.

**Keap:** SMS is available but limited. Two-way SMS requires third-party integration. Much less capable than GoHighLevel.

**Verdict:** GoHighLevel wins by a wide margin.

---

## Appointment Booking

**GoHighLevel:** Full native calendar and booking system. Supports individual and team calendars, round-robin booking, appointment reminders (SMS + email), Google Calendar sync, and Zoom integration.

**Keap:** Has appointment scheduling but it's more basic. Less configuration flexibility.

**Verdict:** GoHighLevel wins.

---

## E-commerce and Payments

**Keap:** Historically strong in this area. Native order management, product catalog, invoicing, payment plans, and e-commerce triggers in automations.

**GoHighLevel:** Has basic e-commerce capabilities (Stripe integration, order forms, subscriptions) but lacks a true product catalog or inventory management.

**Verdict:** Keap wins for pure e-commerce needs.

---

## Reporting and Analytics

**Keap:** Good reporting for email metrics, contact activity, and sales. The Max tier includes more advanced reporting.

**GoHighLevel:** Covers funnel analytics, campaign performance, call reporting, appointment tracking, and ad attribution. Dashboards are customizable.

**Verdict:** GoHighLevel offers more variety; Keap's contact-level reporting is more detailed.

---

## Agency and Multi-Client Features

This is where GoHighLevel wins outright.

**GoHighLevel:**
- Unlimited sub-accounts (on Unlimited plan)
- White-label branding
- Snapshot system for templating
- SaaS Mode for reselling
- Agency master dashboard

**Keap:**
- No sub-account system
- No white-label capability
- No agency management features

If you manage multiple clients, Keap is simply not designed for you.

**Verdict:** GoHighLevel wins — it's not close.

---

## Ease of Use

**Keap:** Historically complex. The original Infusionsoft had a reputation for being notoriously difficult to learn. Keap has simplified things but still has a moderate learning curve.

**GoHighLevel:** Also has a learning curve — there's a lot packed into the platform. New users typically spend 2–4 weeks getting comfortable.

**Verdict:** Neither is beginner-friendly. Keap is slightly simpler for basic CRM. GoHighLevel is more complex but more capable.

---

## Integrations

**Keap:** 2,500+ integrations via Zapier, plus native integrations with major tools. Strong integration ecosystem built over 20+ years.

**GoHighLevel:** 40+ native integrations, plus Zapier support. The native integration count is growing but smaller than Keap's.

**Verdict:** Keap has more integrations due to its longer history.

## Who Should Choose GoHighLevel?

- Marketing agencies managing multiple clients
- Consultants who want to white-label a CRM
- SaaS entrepreneurs building a recurring revenue software product
- Businesses that rely heavily on SMS marketing
- Anyone replacing 3+ separate marketing tools
- Local service businesses wanting an all-in-one stack

## Who Should Choose Keap?

- Small business owners who want a simple CRM without the agency features
- E-commerce businesses needing native product catalog and order management
- Businesses with complex email automation needs and no SMS requirement
- Users who rely on Keap's 20+ years of e-commerce integrations
- Former Infusionsoft (Keap Max Classic) users with deeply built-out campaigns

---

## Final Verdict

For **marketing agencies** and **SaaS builders**, GoHighLevel is the clear winner. It does more, costs less, and offers capabilities Keap simply doesn't have (white-label, sub-accounts, SaaS Mode, native SMS).

For **small businesses** with straightforward CRM needs, no need for multi-account management, and a reliance on e-commerce — Keap remains a capable choice.

The pricing difference alone is significant: GoHighLevel's Agency Unlimited plan at $297/month covers unlimited clients, while Keap's $299/month Pro plan covers just 1,500 contacts at a single company. The value math heavily favors GoHighLevel for any business beyond the most basic use case.
