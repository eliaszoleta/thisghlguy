Cleaning businesses run on repeat clients and referrals — but getting there requires reliable follow-up, confirmed appointments, and a steady stream of online reviews. GoHighLevel automation handles all of it.

Here's exactly how to set up GoHighLevel automation for a cleaning business, from quote request to 5-star Google review.

## Why Automation Matters for Cleaning Businesses

Cleaning businesses are typically owner-operated or small-team operations. The owner is often out cleaning or managing cleaners all day — not sitting by the phone waiting to follow up on a quote request that came in at 2pm.

By the time an owner gets home and follows up at 6pm, the potential client has already booked with someone else.

GoHighLevel automation fixes this by:
- **Responding to quote requests instantly** via SMS and email, even while you're on a job
- **Confirming and reminding appointments** so clients don't forget or cancel last-minute
- **Asking for Google reviews** right after a successful clean, when clients are happiest
- **Re-booking past clients** with automated check-ins so you maintain recurring revenue

## The Cleaning Business Automation System

### Automation 1: Quote Request Follow-Up

**Trigger:** Form submitted (quote request form on your website or landing page)

**Immediate SMS:**
> "Hi [First Name]! This is [Name] from [Business Name]. Just got your cleaning quote request — we'd love to help! Can I ask a few quick questions about your home? Reply YES or grab a time to chat: [calendar link]"

**If no reply in 30 min → Email:**
Subject: "Your cleaning quote request"
> "Hi [First Name], thanks for reaching out to [Business Name]! I'd love to set up a quick call to give you an accurate quote for your home/office. Here's my calendar: [link]. You can also reply to this email with any questions."

**If no reply in 4 hours → Voicemail drop:**
> "Hi [First Name], this is [Name] from [Business Name]. Just following up on your cleaning quote request. Give me a call at [number] or just reply to my text — I'd love to get you a quote!"

**Day 1 → SMS:**
> "Hey [First Name], still here whenever you're ready. Happy to give you a quick quote — it only takes a few minutes. Just reply here or grab a time: [link]"

**Day 3 → Email:**
Subject: "What's included in a [Business Name] clean"
> Detail your cleaning process, what's included, before/after examples, and link to reviews.

**Day 7 → SMS:**
> "Last check-in [First Name] — if you're still looking for a cleaner, we'd love the chance to earn your business. Here's a quick-book link: [calendar link]"

→ After 7 days with no response: Add tag "Cold Cleaning Lead" → long-term monthly follow-up

### Automation 2: Booking Confirmation and Reminders

**Trigger:** Appointment booked

**Immediate SMS:**
> "Hi [First Name], your cleaning with [Business Name] is confirmed! [Date] at [Time]. We'll arrive within a 30-minute window. Questions? Reply here."

**Immediate Email:**
Full booking confirmation with date, time, address, contact info, any pre-cleaning instructions (secure pets, clear counters, etc.), and rescheduling link.

**24 hours before → SMS:**
> "Reminder: [Business Name] will be at your home tomorrow at [Time]. Reply YES to confirm, or call [Number] to reschedule if needed."

**2 hours before → SMS:**
> "We're on our way in about 2 hours, [First Name]! [Business Name] — see you at [Time]."

**If status changes to Cancelled:**
> "Hi [First Name], looks like your cleaning was cancelled. Want to rebook? Here are available times: [link]. Or reply here and I'll help find a time."

### Automation 3: Post-Clean Review Request

**Trigger:** Appointment status changed to "Completed" OR Tag added: "Clean Complete"

**Wait 3 hours → SMS:**
> "Hi [First Name], hope your home is sparkling! The team loved being there. If you have a moment, we'd really appreciate a quick Google review — it means a lot to our small business: [review link]"

**Wait 3 days → If no review → Email:**
Subject: "How did we do?"
> "Hi [First Name], just following up from your recent clean with [Business Name]. If everything went well, would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? [review link]. If anything wasn't right, please let us know and we'll make it right."

**If positive review received → Tag "Left Review" → SMS:**
> "Thank you so much for the review, [First Name]! It means the world to us. Can't wait to see you next time!"

### Automation 4: Recurring Client Re-Booking

For one-time cleaning clients who haven't rebooked, automate a check-in at regular intervals.

**Trigger:** Tag added: "One-Time Clean — No Rebook"

**2 weeks after service → SMS:**
> "Hi [First Name]! It's been a couple weeks since your clean with [Business Name]. Homes get dusty fast! Want to schedule your next one? Reply here or book online: [link]"

**1 month after service → Email:**
Subject: "Time for another clean, [First Name]?"
> "A lot of our clients do monthly or bi-monthly cleans to keep up with the mess. Here's a quick link to check availability: [link]. First-time recurring clients get [X% off / bonus service]."

**3 months after service → SMS:**
> "Hi [First Name], it's [Name] from [Business Name]. Just checking in — it's been a few months since your last clean. Any chance you're looking for regular cleaning service?"

**6 months after → SMS:**
> "Hey [First Name] — long time! We have some availability coming up if you'd like to book a clean. Here's the link: [link]. Hope to hear from you!"

### Automation 5: Referral Request

**Trigger:** Tag added: "Happy Client" (add after receiving a 5-star review or positive feedback)

**Wait 2 days → SMS:**
> "Hi [First Name], so glad you loved your clean! If you know anyone who could use a great cleaner, we'd love an introduction. We'll give you [incentive] for every referral who books. Just reply with their info or have them mention your name when they reach out!"
