GoHighLevel and ClickFunnels are two of the most popular platforms in digital marketing — but they serve very different purposes.

ClickFunnels built its reputation as the go-to funnel builder for entrepreneurs. GoHighLevel came along as an all-in-one agency platform. Today, they compete for many of the same customers.

Which one is right for you? This comparison breaks it down completely.

## GoHighLevel vs ClickFunnels: Pricing

**ClickFunnels 2.0:**
- Basic: $97/month (1 website, 20 funnels)
- Pro: $197/month (3 websites, 100 funnels)
- Funnel Hacker: $297/month (unlimited)

**GoHighLevel:**
- Starter: $97/month (3 sub-accounts, all features)
- Agency Unlimited: $297/month (unlimited sub-accounts, SaaS Mode)
- Agency Pro: $497/month (white-label mobile app)

At the same price point, **GoHighLevel provides significantly more value**. For $97/month, GHL includes SMS marketing, a full CRM, reputation management, and booking calendars — none of which are in ClickFunnels' $97 plan.

## Funnel Builder: How Do They Compare?

ClickFunnels was *built* for funnels. Its funnel builder is intuitive, with a clean drag-and-drop interface and a large library of proven templates. It remains one of the best pure funnel experiences available.

GoHighLevel's funnel builder is capable and covers all the same ground — opt-in pages, sales pages, order forms, upsells, downsells, and thank-you pages. It's slightly less polished than ClickFunnels' builder, but it's gotten significantly better with each update.

**Winner for pure funnel building:** ClickFunnels (marginally)
**Winner for funnels + everything else:** GoHighLevel

## CRM: GoHighLevel Wins Clearly

ClickFunnels 2.0 added basic contact management, but it's nowhere close to a real CRM. GoHighLevel's CRM is a core feature built to handle:

- Full contact timelines and activity history
- Pipeline stages with drag-and-drop deal management
- Contact tagging and segmentation
- Integrated communication (email, SMS, call logs)
- Lead scoring and smart lists

If you're managing sales conversations or client relationships, there is no comparison — GoHighLevel's CRM is far superior.

## Automation: A Major Gap

ClickFunnels has basic automation (follow-up sequences tied to funnel actions), but GoHighLevel's workflow builder is in a different league. GHL lets you build complex, multi-step automation that spans email, SMS, calls, voicemail drops, and internal actions.

The workflow trigger options in GHL alone — form filled, appointment booked, tag added, deal stage changed, payment received — give you granular control that ClickFunnels simply can't match.

**Winner: GoHighLevel, decisively.**

## Email Marketing Comparison

Both platforms include email marketing. GoHighLevel's email system covers broadcasts, automation sequences, and a visual builder. ClickFunnels 2.0's email system is comparable, with a solid drag-and-drop builder and automation.

This one is roughly even. Power users may find GHL's email deliverability tooling more configurable, but for most users either platform works.

## SMS Marketing: GoHighLevel Only

ClickFunnels has no native SMS. GoHighLevel has two-way SMS built directly in — not as an add-on or integration, but as a core feature with its own inbox, automation triggers, and broadcast system.

For any business that relies on text message follow-ups (which is most modern marketing), this is a significant advantage for GHL.

**Winner: GoHighLevel.**

## Multi-Client / Agency Management

ClickFunnels has no real multi-client management. Each account is for one business.

GoHighLevel was designed specifically for agencies. Sub-accounts give each client their own isolated environment. You manage everything from one dashboard. You can white-label the platform. You can resell it as SaaS.

**Winner: GoHighLevel, by a wide margin.**

## Ease of Use

ClickFunnels is simpler and cleaner. The UI is more polished, and first-time users typically get a funnel live faster.

GoHighLevel has a steeper learning curve. The sheer volume of features can be overwhelming at first. However, once you learn the platform, it becomes significantly more powerful.

**Winner for simplicity:** ClickFunnels
**Winner for power users:** GoHighLevel

## Who Should Choose ClickFunnels?

- Solo entrepreneurs selling courses, ebooks, or digital products
- Info-product creators who live and breathe funnels
- People who want the simplest possible setup
- Those who don't need multi-client management or SMS

## Who Should Choose GoHighLevel?

- Marketing agencies managing multiple clients
- Businesses that need a real CRM + automation combination
- Entrepreneurs who want to build a white-label SaaS
- Local businesses that need reputation management, booking, and follow-up
- Anyone currently paying for both a CRM and ClickFunnels (GHL replaces both)

## The Final Verdict

If you're a solo entrepreneur who just wants to build funnels with minimal complexity, ClickFunnels is a fine choice.

But if you're running an agency, managing clients, or want to build something more comprehensive — **GoHighLevel is the clear winner**. It does everything ClickFunnels does, plus adds a complete CRM, SMS, automation, reputation management, booking, and white-label capabilities.

At the same price, you simply get more with GoHighLevel.
